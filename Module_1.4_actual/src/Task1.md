## Question 1
Using an example case (health centre, bank, etc.), discuss the meaning and characterisation of all the performance measures discussed in the 
lesson material using the concepts of the target system.


### Answer
I want to use an example of a hair salon. 

My variables are :

### Performance Variables – Hair Salon Simulation

- A – Arrived customers:Number of customers who arrived at the salon – 45.
- C – Customers served: Number of customers helped by the stylist – 3.
- B – Active service time: Total time the stylist spent actively serving customers – 20 minutes.
- T – Total simulation time:How long the simulation ran – 60 minutes.
- U – Utilization:How busy the stylist was.  
  U = B / T = 20 / 60 = 0.33 
- X –  Number of customers served per unit of time.  
  X = C / T = 3 / 60 = 0.05 customers per minute
- S – Average service time: Average time the stylist actively spent serving each customer.  
  S = B / C = 20 / 3 = 6.67 minutes
- Rᵢ – Individual response time: Time from when an individual customer joins the checks in until they exit
    - Customer 1: 30 minutes
    - Customer 2: 30 minutes
    - Customer 3: 60 minutes
- W – Total response time: 
  W = 30 + 30 + 60 = 120 minutes
- R – Average response time:
  R = W / C = 120 / 3 = 40
- N – Average number of customers:  
  N = W / T = 120 / 60 = 2

The hair salon simulation runs for one hour (60 minutes) total. In this time 45 customers arrived, checked in, and entered the queue to get 
their hair done by the stylist.However, the stylist could only help 3 customers today. The stylist began services at 10am and took customer number one who arrived at 10 am, checked in and then got a hair cut, paid, and left all in 30 minutes. 
After this, customers 2 and 3  followed suit and received a hair wash and dry in their corresponding times. 
The active service time or the actual time spent working with customers was 20 minutes. The stylist was seen to be busy with utilization or working rate of 33.3%.
The stylist served 0.05 customers per minute of the simulation and the average time spent working with people was 6.67 minutes. The total response time of all customers was 120 minutes. 
On average the queue held 2 people throughout the 60  minute simulation and the average response time of those who were helped was 40 minutes. This included the customers entry, hair services, and exit.
